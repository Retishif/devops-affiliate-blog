# Contact

For corrections, technical feedback, or suggestions for future guides, please [open a new GitHub issue](https://github.com/Retishif/devops-affiliate-blog/issues/new).

When reporting a technical issue, include:

- The page URL and section heading
- The relevant tool or provider version
- The command, configuration, or behavior you observed
- A link to supporting documentation, if available

Please do not include credentials, access tokens, private keys, or other secrets in public issues.
