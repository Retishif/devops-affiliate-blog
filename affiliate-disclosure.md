# Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you follow an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase or sign up for a service, the site may receive a commission. This does not add a fee to your purchase unless the provider explicitly states otherwise.

Affiliate relationships do not guarantee that a product will be recommended. Technical recommendations should be based on suitability, documented capabilities, operational trade-offs, and the reader's requirements.

## How to identify affiliate links

Where affiliate links are used, the surrounding text or link will identify the commercial relationship. Links may also use the appropriate rel="sponsored" attribute in the site's rendered HTML.

Cloud infrastructure pricing, service limits, product features, and affiliate-program terms can change. Always check the provider's current documentation, pricing page, and terms before making a decision.
