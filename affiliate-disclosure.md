# Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

**Advertising Disclosure:** Cloud Native Craft is reader-supported. When you buy or sign up through links on this site, we may earn an affiliate commission or advertising fee at no additional cost to you. Opinions and technical evaluations remain our own.

A product's affiliate program doesn't decide whether we recommend it. Recommendations are based on how well a tool fits the job, what it can actually do, the operational trade-offs, and your requirements.

## How to identify affiliate links

Affiliate links are labeled “(affiliate)” in the text or covered by a disclosure note nearby. They also carry the `rel="sponsored"` attribute in the page's HTML, so search engines can tell they're paid placements.

## Verify before you buy

Cloud pricing, service limits, product features, and affiliate-program terms change. Check the provider's current documentation, pricing page, and terms before you decide.
